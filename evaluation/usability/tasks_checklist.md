# Usability tasks and observer checklist

Give each participant the scenario first, then one task card at a time. Card wording
is in quotes; success criteria are for the observer only.

**Scenario (read aloud):** "You worked at Kenya Airways for several years and have
since moved on. A former colleague told you about Lumina, a network for former KQ
staff. You have just opened it for the first time."

**Fictional registry card (hand to the participant for T2):** a card printed from one
unclaimed row of `data/synthetic_alumni_registry.csv`: staff number, surname, former
department, start and end year. Use a different row for each participant, and check
it is still unclaimed (Admin › Users) before the session.

| # | Task card | Success criteria (observer) | Feature evaluated |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| T1 | "Create your Lumina account or sign in with Google." | Reaches the verification step signed in | Google / email sign-up |
| T2 | "Confirm that you are a former Kenya Airways employee, using the details on your card." | Verification accepted; lands on the profile page | Registry verification |
| T3 | "Complete your profile: add your former KQ role, department and years, your current job, and three skills." | Profile saved with all listed fields; profile strength rises | Profile editor |
| T4 | "You want to move from airline work into hotel management. Use Lumina to find someone who could mentor you, and open the profile of the person you would contact first." | Uses the natural-language mentor search; opens a mentor profile from the results | Semantic mentor matching |
| T5 | "Why did Lumina suggest that person? Tell me in your own words." | Participant finds and paraphrases the "Why this match" reasons | Grounded explanations |
| T6 | "Ask that person to mentor you." | Mentorship request sent with a message | Mentorship requests |
| T7 | "Find a former colleague who worked in your old department and send them a connection invitation." | Uses Network search or department filter; invitation sent | Alumni directory, connections |
| T8 | "Find an upcoming online event that interests you and reserve a place." | RSVP confirmed; event shows as attending | Events |
| T9 | "Find a real job in Kenya that fits your background and open its application page." | Opens Opportunities › Jobs in Kenya, uses search or a topic, follows an Apply link | Kenya job feed |
| T10 | "You would rather not appear in the alumni directory for now. Change that, then download a copy of your data." | Directory visibility off; data export downloaded | Privacy controls |

Optional, only for participants acting as an administrator:

| # | Task card | Success criteria | Feature evaluated |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A1 | "A member asked for help with verification. Find their request and resolve it." | Opens Verification Exceptions and resolves the request | Admin verification |
| A2 | "Check whether every part of the system is working." | Opens System health and explains what it shows | Admin health |
